How to measure

  • Wrap the tape around the fullest part of your chest, under the arms, and keep it level.
  • Between two sizes, pick the larger for shirts and the smaller for tees.
  • Trousers come with a 32 in inseam. Hemming is free at the store.
  • Send your chest and height on WhatsApp and we will suggest a size.
